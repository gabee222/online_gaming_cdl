Online Gaming

Online gaming is defined as playing an electronic game with some visual interface and input device to generate visual feedback on a screen, interacting with people on the internet. On this page, we will analyze how people participate in online gaming in daily life, how someone personally gets involved in online gaming (and how I personally interacted with it), and how we can consider each part of the term Critical Digital Literacy (CDL).

Throughout this course, we have analyzed quite a few news articles, videos, and podcasts that talk about video gaming. Specifically, I am going to go through Co-playing: video games (NYTimes article), Gaming has benefits and perils - parents can help kids by playing with them, and a few related articles that bring concepts that are useful for interpreting our digestion of video games. These artifacts talk about how these forms of media, sometimes naming video gaming specifically, help us understand, critically think, and participate in technology as a whole.

Definition

What do we know about video gaming?

Online gaming has become quite a phenomenon in the past decade. While being a specific niche hobby in the past, it has become a widespread pastime that everyone, especially the younger generation, engages in while resting at home. Therefore, even more so in the modern-era, it is important to think critically about how we are engaging in this media. Understanding the benefits and disadvantages of online gaming is important to the way our society develops and evolves.

Daily Life

How do people engage with online gaming in their daily lives?

Online gaming is usually an activity that people do after class or work. One of the benefits of online gaming is that you can hangout and have fun with your friends from anywhere in the world, meaning that when you go home and just want to relax, it is easy to just play some online games with your friends. People can also online game together, in-person. In one case, as seen in Co-playing: video games, online gaming can be a fun way to teach kids about important concepts like problem solving and collaboration.
